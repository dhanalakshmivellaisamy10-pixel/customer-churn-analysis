# customer-churn-analysis
Customer churn analysis in Excel: churn drivers, interaction effects and revenue impact
## Key findings
- Customers with an international plan and more than 3 service calls churn at 76.47%.
- Churned customers have a higher average bill (65.53) than customers who stayed (58.46).
- Churned customers account for 15.51% of total billing.
- Estimated lifetime value lost from 598 churned customers is about 4.00 million.

## Methods
COUNTIFS, SUMIF, AVERAGEIF, churn rate by segment, interaction effects, revenue impact.
